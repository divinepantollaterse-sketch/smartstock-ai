# SmartStock AI
### An AI-Powered Inventory Assistant for Small Businesses

**Building AI course project**

## Summary

SmartStock AI is a proposed artificial intelligence tool that helps small business owners monitor their inventory, predict product demand, and identify which products need restocking. It aims to make inventory management easier, faster, and more organized.

## Background

Managing inventory is one of the common challenges faced by small business owners. Some businesses experience product shortages because they do not monitor their stocks regularly, while others have excess products that remain unsold for a long time.

This project aims to address the following problems:

- Difficulty monitoring available products and stock levels.
- Unexpected product shortages.
- Excess inventory and unsold products.
- Lack of proper information when deciding which products to restock.

I chose this idea because inventory management is important in running a successful business. With the help of AI, small business owners can make better decisions and manage their products more efficiently.

## How Is It Used?

SmartStock AI is designed for small business owners, sari-sari stores, grocery stores, and other small retail businesses.

The users can enter information about their products, available stock, and daily sales. The AI system will analyze the information and provide suggestions about which products may need restocking based on previous sales and inventory records.

The system can be used to:

- Monitor available product stocks.
- Identify products that are frequently purchased.
- Predict possible product demand.
- Provide restocking suggestions.
- Help prevent unnecessary excess inventory.

## Data Sources and AI Methods

SmartStock AI will depend on inventory records, daily sales reports, product information, and previous sales history provided by participating small businesses.

The project may use the following AI techniques:

- **Data Analysis:** To examine sales records and identify product trends.
- **Machine Learning:** To learn from previous sales patterns and predict future product demand.
- **Classification:** To categorize products according to their stock levels, such as low stock, sufficient stock, or excess stock.

The quality and accuracy of the results will depend on the completeness and reliability of the collected data.

## Challenges

Although SmartStock AI can help improve inventory management, it also has some limitations.

- Incorrect or incomplete sales records may affect the accuracy of predictions.
- Some small businesses may not have organized digital inventory records.
- The system may not accurately predict sudden changes in customer demand.
- Users may need basic knowledge of digital tools to operate the system.
- Data privacy and the protection of business information must be considered.

SmartStock AI will serve as a support tool and will not completely replace the decisions of business owners.

## What Next?

In the future, SmartStock AI could be improved by adding more features, such as automatic inventory updates, sales reports, and notifications when products are running low.

It could also be developed into a mobile application to make it more accessible to small business owners. With further development, testing, and reliable data, the project could become a useful tool for improving inventory management in small businesses.
